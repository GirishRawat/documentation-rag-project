Car displays provide drivers and front-seat passengers many ways to engage with your app - from driving-optimized experiences like navigation or listening to music and podcasts, to parked experiences like video streaming and playing games.

Here, you'll find all the tools and guidance you need to bring the best of your app to the car.

## Create inspiring in-car experiences

### Media

Entertain drivers and passengers with music, podcasts, and audiobooks on the go.
[Design a media app](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/app-types/media-apps)

### Navigation

Help people find and explore more on the go, like new places to play, fuel, charge, and shop.
[Design a navigation app](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/app-types/navigation-apps)

### Communications

Design seamless calling and messaging experiences for the car.
[Design a communications app](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/app-types/communications)

### Do more on the go

Enable users to do more with your app in the car, from checking the weather to browsing the web and beyond.
[Learn more](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/app-types/other-apps)

### Parked experiences that entertain

Keep everyone in the car engaged with parked experiences including web browsing, games like Angry Birds 2 and Farm Heroes Saga, and more.
[Design for parked experiences](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/app-types/parked-passenger-apps)

## Get started

### Design principles

App content and interactions should complement the driving or parked experience and minimize distraction while driving.
[See design principles](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/foundations/design-principles)

### Design process

Learn the design foundations for Android for Cars, including key use cases and concepts.
[See design process](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/foundations/design-process)

### UX requirements

Make sure your app meets design requirements for usability in cars.
[See UX requirements](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/ux-requirements/overview)

### Templates

Use templates as guardrails to help you customize driving app experiences while Google provides the foundations for your app.
[See templates](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/templates/overview)

### Components

Templates include components that act as building blocks for a consistent user experience across all apps for cars.
[See components](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/components/overview)

### Flows, behaviors, and patterns

Reuse proven design patterns and behaviors so drivers can quickly complete tasks in the car.
[See behaviors](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/guides/flows/overview)

### Design kit

Browse examples of Android Auto apps to help you get inspired.
[See the design kit](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/cars/samples)
