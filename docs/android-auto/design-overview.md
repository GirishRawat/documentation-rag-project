## Welcome to Design for Driving

Design for Driving serves as the central design resource for developers and automotive manufacturers building for two Android platforms:

- **Android Auto**: A phone-based infotainment system projected onto vehicle screens
- **Android Automotive OS (AAOS)**: A customizable infotainment platform that manufacturers can integrate into vehicles

The site offers design guidance for both systems. For technical implementation details, refer to the Android for Cars documentation.

## Create Apps for Cars

### General Design Guidance

Foundational design principles for vehicle interfaces include:

- Interaction design principles
- Visual design principles
- Style foundations

### Android Auto Apps

Supported app categories include communication, media, navigation, and other driving-related applications like IoT and point-of-interest services.

### Automotive OS Apps

Development categories encompass communication, media, navigation, driving-related apps, and parked/passenger experiences such as video streaming.

## Design an In-Vehicle Infotainment System

Resources for system designers provide quick access to design principles, style information, and layout guidance.

### Interaction Models for AAOS

Key interaction models covered include dialer functionality, media templates, notifications, and calendar applications.
