CarPlay is a smarter, safer way to use iPhone in the car. CarPlay apps let people get directions, send and receive messages, play music, and more, all while staying focused on the road. And if you're an automaker, you can support CarPlay Ultra for the ultimate iPhone experience in the car.

## What's New

- Create CarPlay video apps so people can browse and watch their favorite content in supported cars when parked
- Enhance your CarPlay app with new template options in the CarPlay framework
- CarPlay navigation apps can display enhanced information panels and share route information with supported cars

## CarPlay for App Developers

Whether a car supports CarPlay or CarPlay Ultra, you can enable your apps on the driver's screens by supporting the CarPlay framework.

### Apps

- **Audio apps** can play music, podcasts, and more
- **Video apps** can play video content in supported cars when parked
- **Messaging and VoIP apps** that support SiriKit intents can be updated to appear in CarPlay
- **Navigation apps** can provide turn-by-turn directions and show guidance in the instrument cluster or head-up display
- **Special-purpose apps** for EV charging, fueling, parking, public safety, quick food ordering, voice-based conversational apps, and driving task apps can help people accomplish important tasks without taking out their phone
- **Widgets and Live Activities** work seamlessly to deliver timely information without drawing focus from the road
- CarPlay audio and video apps also work on Apple HomeView without requiring modifications

### Widgets and Live Activities

Display important information and real-time updates in CarPlay with glanceable widgets and Live Activities:

- Widgets appear to the left of CarPlay Dashboard and support interaction on touchscreen vehicles
- Live Activities are automatically shown in CarPlay Dashboard, or as a notification
- Small widgets or Live Activities automatically appear in CarPlay with no changes required
- If your widget is not functional or suitable for use in the car, you can indicate that it's not optimized for CarPlay to prevent it from being shown

The CarPlay Developer Guide contains details on when to specify widgets aren't optimized for CarPlay.

### Tools and Resources

- Download additional tools for Xcode to access the CarPlay simulator and easily replicate a CarPlay environment from your Mac
- Download the CarPlay Developer Guide to learn about supported CarPlay app categories and details about templates used to develop CarPlay apps
- Explore design guidelines to find out how to optimize your app's UI and interactions for CarPlay
- Request CarPlay app entitlement to let Apple know if your app has the potential to be supported by CarPlay

## CarPlay for Automakers

Update your vehicle system to support CarPlay, or enable the ultimate iPhone experience with CarPlay Ultra. By supporting video in the car, people can watch their favorite content when they aren't driving.

### CarPlay

CarPlay works with your car's hardware and supports a wide range of screen configurations, including touchscreens, touch pads, and knob interfaces.

Enable a great iPhone experience in your car by supporting CarPlay features such as:

- Wireless connectivity
- Multitouch
- Dynamic screen sizing
- Always-on Siri
- Route guidance in your instrument cluster or head-up display
- Spatial Audio for multi-channel audio support

### CarPlay Ultra

CarPlay Ultra is the ultimate iPhone experience for the car:

- Combine your car's signature features, branding, and design with an Apple look and feel for a unified and consistent appearance
- Provide content for all the driver's screens, including the instrument cluster
- Completely wireless experience that appears as soon as your car's displays turn on
- Relies on the driver's iPhone and is available when their device is present
- Driving features are still powered by your built-in system to ensure robustness and smooth action

### Additional Capabilities

#### Automaker Apps

Apps published by automakers let drivers control car features without ever leaving CarPlay. You can also use apps to extend the capabilities of CarPlay Ultra for features that are unique to your cars. For electric vehicles, support EnergyKit in your app so people can optimize electricity usage at home.

#### Video in the Car

AirPlay video in the car enables people to watch their favorite videos from iPhone right on their CarPlay display when they aren't driving. Integrate support for CarPlay with AirPlay video to enable this feature in your car.

#### Car Keys

With digital car keys, people can unlock and start their car with iPhone or Apple Watch. You can update your vehicle system to support car keys.

If you are interested in supporting CarPlay, CarPlay Ultra, AirPlay video, or car keys in your vehicle system, learn more about the [MFi Program](https://mfi.apple.com/).
